about me

I’m a Junior Data Engineer at Natixis with a background in Electrical and Computer Engineering and hands-on experience designing ETL pipelines, SQL (Apache Hive) and data quality within banking.

In the CIB Financing Data Team, I design and maintain ETL pipelines in EasyMorph and SQL (Apache Hive), transforming multi-source data into the reporting-ready datasets behind approximately 300 financial reports. I maintain and validate more than 30 Hive tables used as the reference source for financing data across the company, and implement data quality controls such as row-count comparisons, totals reconciliation and outlier detection.

I migrated approximately 80 reports from IBM Cognos to EasyMorph, rewriting and optimizing SQL logic and validating outputs against the legacy source. Previously, I built an automated Python ETL pipeline with Pandas and NumPy that replaced legacy PowerShell workflows and cut processing time by roughly 90%, ingesting and standardizing more than 150 CSV, Excel, TXT and JSON files.

I’m focused on progressing further into Data Engineering, building foundational knowledge in PySpark and Medallion Architecture, and currently learning Databricks, Apache Airflow, dbt and Docker.
